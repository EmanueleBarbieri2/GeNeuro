# Leakage-free baseline results

Initial nested five-fold results produced on 2026-09-28 with seed 42 are shown
below. Model and regularization choices were selected by three-fold stratified
CV inside each outer training partition. These are out-of-fold test metrics,
not best-validation metrics.

| Task | Best baseline | Balanced accuracy | Macro-F1 | ROC-AUC |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AD vs MCI vs CN | fMRI logistic regression | 0.530 ± 0.063 | 0.531 ± 0.067 | 0.745 ± 0.042 macro OvR |
| AD vs CN | fMRI logistic regression | 0.716 ± 0.117 | 0.710 ± 0.123 | 0.794 ± 0.063 |
| MCI vs CN | early-fusion PCA + logistic regression | 0.702 ± 0.031 | 0.701 ± 0.032 | 0.764 ± 0.025 |
| AD vs MCI | fMRI PCA + logistic regression | 0.605 ± 0.045 | 0.601 ± 0.050 | 0.651 ± 0.079 |

For AD vs CN, the best fMRI logistic model additionally obtained accuracy
0.806 ± 0.088, PR-AUC 0.516 ± 0.140, AD sensitivity 0.569 ± 0.170, and CN
specificity 0.863 ± 0.075. Accuracy must be read against the 80.2% CN-majority
baseline; balanced accuracy and ROC-AUC are the more informative results.

These results establish that the matrices contain appreciable diagnostic
signal and that fMRI currently carries more of it than DTI. They also provide
the reference threshold that neural models must exceed under the same outer
folds. Full run artifacts are generated by `run_classical_baselines.py` as
`summary.json`, `fold_metrics.csv`, and `predictions.csv`.
